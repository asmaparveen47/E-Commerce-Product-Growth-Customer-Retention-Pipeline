# Retail Analytics & Loyalty Funnel Optimization


📌 The Problem I Solved

A major retail store noticed that its customers' shopping habits were changing across different age groups, seasons, and sales channels. They had mountains of receipt data but didn't know how to use it. They tasked me with analyzing this data to figure out exactly what drives sales, what keeps customers coming back, and how they can optimize their marketing and inventory strategies.


My Goal: I built a clean, interactive two-page Power BI dashboard to turn this messy transaction data into clear, visual answers for the management team.

📊 The Core Insights I Discovered

• The Seasonal Goldmine: Fall is the primary revenue engine ($60K revenue / $61.6 AOV), while Summer marks the definitive commercial low point ($56K revenue / $58.4 AOV).


• The Retention Paradox: We boast an elite 97.9% initial customer retention rate (3,817 out of 3,900 shoppers return).


• The Subscription Leak: A massive 72.4% loyalty drop-off happens at the bottom of the funnel, leaving 50% of the entire database (1,962 shoppers) classified as "Loyal" but completely unsubscribed.


• Deal Breakers & Shipping Friction: Generic coupon promotions decrease baseline customer satisfaction (CSAT drops from 42.38 to 41.26).


• Fulfillment Bottlenecks: Free Shipping scored the worst (39.26 CSAT) compared to Standard Shipping (46.64 CSAT), proving free delivery partners are way too slow.

# 🛠️ How I Structured the Dashboard


# Page 1: Strategic Revenue Overview (The Big Picture)

• KPI Banner: Lined up four quick cards across the top tracking total revenue, shopper counts, checkout baselines, and satisfaction levels.


• Demographics & Products: Built visuals showing that Adults ($68K) spend the most total cash, while Clothing ($104K) is our massive, runaway top-performing product category.


• Payment Channels: Found that Credit Cards, PayPal, and Cash ($40K each) are neck-and-neck as the preferred ways people like to pay.

# Page 2: Loyalty Pipeline & Customer Experience (The "Why")

• Customer Maturity Funnel: A clean, descending chart showing exactly how many buyers progress from their first visit to becoming paid members.


• Fulfillment Matrices: Simple grids mapping customer happiness directly against shipping speeds and discount usage to highlight business friction.


# 🚀 The Action Plan I Recommended to Management

• Target the Unsubscribed Loyal Shoppers: Focus on migrating the massive 50% "Loyal-No" subscriber segment into the membership program using convenience perks (like automatic monthly delivery) instead of profit-wasting discount codes.


• Fix Free Shipping: Audit our free delivery methods immediately to speed up transit times and rescue our lowest-performing fulfillment satisfaction score.


• Stop Store-Wide Discounting: Phase out generic coupons since the data proves they hurt customer satisfaction while needlessly eating away at net profits.
